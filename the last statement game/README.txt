THE LAST STATEMENT
Offline choice-based detective game

HOW TO PLAY
1. Keep the 'scenes' folder next to index.html.
2. Double-click index.html to play in a modern browser.
3. Use mouse choices or Arrow Up/Down + Enter.
4. Press E to open the evidence board.
5. Progress is saved automatically in your browser.
